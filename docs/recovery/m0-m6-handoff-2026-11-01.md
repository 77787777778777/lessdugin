## Final M6 fixes

The final review/fix passes covered:

- Live-frame replay preserving the complete output, including
  `OutdatedCursor` restart semantics. Two endpoints cannot acknowledge one
  another's pending frame, and cursors advance only after actions succeed.
- Candidate persistence and migration, rejection back-off, recency, and
  identical-CID leave/rejoin reconfirmation.
- Targeted live identity invalidation, account-status checks, and scoped
  repository resyncs. Failed status checks prevent acknowledgement.
- Per-record PDS fallback after proof-service transport failures.
- `BadSignature` below a fresh PDS head is handled as stale-proof: fetch a
  fresh author-PDS proof, preserve proof-level admission, and do not strike the
  indexer. Current-head forgery still counts against the source.
- Cold first-page PDS fallback when hydration fails **or no usable indexer is
  available**, gated by `discComplete` and a shared fallback lock. Cursor pages
  and already-discovered forums remain local reads.
- Indexer candidate/discovery retry behavior, native/WASM parity, scoped
  indexer consent, CLI/TUI fixture corrections, and bounded native async
  chains.

Indexer responses remain URI/order hints. Local folds compute displayed
content, state, scores, and ordering. Direct mode does not contact indexers.
